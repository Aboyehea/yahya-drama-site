The Story

A broke underdog fencer and the undefeated Olympic favourite fake a romance to destroy the man who betrayed her — and the lie becomes the only true thing in both their lives.

Set in the Olympic fencing world of Cairo and Dubai, ENEMY follows Mohamed Adel and Layla Fahmy through sixty episodes of rivalry, revenge, and a love neither of them planned.

Characters

Mohamed Adel — 28

The underdog. Broke, stubborn, and tired of losing. A small scar on his left eyebrow, a silver watch on his left wrist, and a patience that is running out.

Layla Fahmy — 26

The undefeated Olympic favourite. Cold, proud, controlled — and always with a touch of crimson. Her high ponytail and green-hazel eyes are known across the fencing world.

Karim Shawky — 31

The man who betrayed her. A navy suit, a gold watch, and a half-smirk — the target of the lie.

Nour — 25

Layla’s best friend — and the traitor. A cream silk blouse, a warm smile, and a secret.

Captain Hany — 55

The mentor. Grey stubble, red tracksuit, and the coach who forces them back onto the same piste.
