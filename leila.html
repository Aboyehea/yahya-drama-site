The Story

Leila Mansour arrives at an old 1936 apartment building in downtown Cairo to live in her missing uncle’s apartment. Exploring it, she finds a mysterious message warning her: never use the elevator after 2 a.m.

Unknown to her, someone has been waiting for her arrival for years.

ليلى منصور توصل عمارة قديمة من سنة 1936 في وسط البلد عشان تعيش في شقة عمها المختفي — وتلاقي رسالة غامضة بتحذرها: متستخدميش الأسانسير بعد الساعة 2 بالليل. وهي مش عارفة إن في حد مستني وصولها من سنين.

Characters

Leila Mansour — 28

Light wheat skin, hazel brown eyes, shoulder-length dark brown wavy hair, a beauty mark under her left eye. Beige trench coat, black turtleneck, antique silver watch, brown leather bag.

Adam — 35

Olive skin, short slicked-back black hair, light stubble, a thin scar above his right eyebrow. Charcoal shirt, black leather jacket, a silver ring — and an antique brass key.

Emad — 68

The old caretaker. Grey-white hair, thick grey moustache, slightly hunched, missing his left little finger. A dark brown wool cardigan, a small brown cap, a large keychain, and an old flashlight.
