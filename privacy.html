1. What we collect

This website itself does not ask visitors for personal information and does not run accounts, comments, or newsletters. Standard server logs (such as IP address, browser, and pages visited) may be kept briefly by the hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

The Yahya Drama publishing app, used only by the studio, works with TikTok’s Login Kit and Content Posting API. When the studio’s TikTok account owner authorises it, TikTok provides the app with: the account’s basic profile information needed to confirm the authorised account, and an access token that allows the app to upload the studio’s own videos to that account. The app does not collect or store the account password.

2. How we use it

We use the TikTok authorisation only to upload videos the studio chooses to publish to its own TikTok account (@yehea_drama), and to confirm that the upload succeeded. We do not use it to read private messages, followers’ data, or any other account’s content.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or other third parties. We do not publish on anyone else’s TikTok account. We do not collect data from children through this website or app.

4. Data storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and used only for the uploads described above. The account owner can revoke the app’s access at any time from TikTok’s settings, which immediately stops the app from posting.

5. Third-party services

Video hosting and distribution run through YouTube and TikTok, which collect data under their own privacy policies when you watch there. Links on this site take you to those platforms.

6. Your rights and contact

If you have a privacy question or request about this website or app, email pubgtrawry123@gmail.com and we will respond. If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change.
